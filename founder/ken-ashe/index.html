Ken Ashe

Founder and operator of Lucky Domains. A CPA and PMP with roughly two decades in finance, product, and program leadership, and about 20 years working with websites.

Background

Builder, operator, accountant.

Ken started his career in accounting and financial reporting before moving into project, product, and program leadership. Over roughly two decades, that career ran through S&P Global, EY, Noom, and Prudential Financial, where he served as Director of Project and Product Management.

He has also worked with HTML, CSS, websites, and WordPress for about 20 years. More recently, he has increasingly used AI-assisted development to build websites and applications, including this site and KenAshe.ai.

He built Lucky Domains alongside that career and still runs it himself. The person on your first call is the person who values the domain, scopes the site, runs the audit, and writes the report.

A CPA would call that a control. A project manager would call it a repeatable process.
