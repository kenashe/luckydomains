Presence map

  • Domain The name everything else points back to. Ownership and control, A name that fits the brand, Email on the same name.
  • Website Your own account of who you are, on your own domain. Clear design, Useful content, Speed and accessibility.
  • Search Pages that search engines can read, understand, and show for relevant searches. Pages search engines can find, What customers search for, Local listings and reviews.
  • Profiles Official profiles and structured data that agree with each other. Official profiles, Founders, Structured data.
  • Authority Credible sources, beyond your own site, that describe you accurately. Mentions and citations, Independent references, Your own expertise.
  • AI systems AI assistants and AI-powered search, which may draw on signals like these when they describe a brand. How AI describes you today, The sources it cites, Questions it cannot yet answer.
