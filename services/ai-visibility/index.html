The goal

Make your business easy to understand and easy to verify.

Lucky Domains helps make a company:

  • Easier to understand
  • Easier to verify
  • Associated with the correct people, services, and locations
  • Supported by useful first-party content
  • Consistently represented across trusted profiles and references
  • Measured with emerging AI-citation data where it exists

What AI systems may draw on

  • Crawlable pages with useful first-party content
  • Structured data that matches the visible facts
  • Consistent names, descriptions, and founders across profiles
  • Credible third-party references
  • Clear relationships between people, services, and places

What we look at

The work starts with what AI systems say today, and what they base it on.

  • Brand-related queries

    The questions people ask about your business and your category.

  • AI-generated descriptions

    How major AI assistants describe the business today, and whether it is accurate.

  • Source and citation patterns

    Which sources those answers lean on, and where your brand is missing or misdescribed.

  • First-party content quality

    Whether your own pages say who you are, what you do, where, and for whom.

  • Important unanswered questions

    Questions a customer would ask that nothing on your site answers.

  • External authority signals

    Credible third-party references that corroborate what you say about yourself.

What the work involves

From a written baseline to monitoring.

Where data exists, it is used. Bing Webmaster Tools reports cited pages and grounding queries for Copilot and Bing AI answers. For other systems, the same important questions are re-asked on a schedule and the answers compared with the baseline.

Baseline

Test the questions that matter to your business across major AI systems and record what they say, and which sources they cite.

Consistency audit

Compare your name, description, founders, services, and locations across your site and profiles. List every disagreement.

Content clarity and coverage

Your own pages reviewed for the facts a system needs to explain you.

Source analysis

Find the sources AI systems lean on for your category, and where your brand is missing or misdescribed.

Credibility plan

A plan to strengthen your online credibility: the content on your own site and the credible references that would support it.

Monitoring over time

The baseline re-run on a schedule, with changes reported.

How this differs from Entity SEO. SEO improves the technical and content foundations that support being found. Entity SEO establishes a clear, consistent, verifiable identity. AI visibility improves the content and evidence that search engines and AI systems use when they describe, cite, or answer questions about that identity. See Entity SEO & Digital Identity.

What the sources say

Built on search fundamentals. Measurable, in part.

Source: Google Search Central

Google says the same SEO fundamentals apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode: crawlable pages, useful first-party content, internal links, good page experience, and structured data that matches the visible text. It also says there is no special schema and no new AI text file required.

Read the source
Source: Bing Webmaster Tools

Bing Webmaster Tools now includes an AI Performance report that shows which pages are cited in AI-generated answers in Copilot and Bing, and the grounding queries behind those citations. AI visibility is becoming measurable, at least in part.

Read the source

Realistic expectations. We set realistic expectations and measure progress transparently. No one can guarantee rankings, AI citations, mentions in a particular AI system, or a Knowledge Panel, and we do not. We improve the evidence those systems work from and show you what changed.

Questions

AI visibility, answered.

No one can promise that. What can be done is to make the evidence about your business clear, consistent, and easy to verify, so systems that look for it find it.

It builds on SEO. Crawlable pages, good content, and accurate structured data are the foundation. AI visibility adds entity consistency, third-party corroboration, and testing how AI systems actually describe you.

Google says its search features do not use them and that no special markup is needed. Other services may. Lucky Domains will tell you which, if any, are worth maintaining for your situation.
