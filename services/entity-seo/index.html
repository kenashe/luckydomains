What entity SEO is

Your website can rank and Google can still misunderstand who you are.

Traditional SEO helps pages rank. Entity SEO helps search engines understand the person or organization behind those pages: the canonical website, official profiles, founders, companies, authors, credentials, products, and credible references that all belong to the same real-world entity.

When those signals conflict or remain disconnected, Google may struggle to distinguish one person or company from another. When they are consistent and machine-readable, Google has a clearer entity graph to work with.

This is the structured-identity end of the SEO work Lucky Domains already does. It sits on the same foundation: a real site on a real domain, clean technical SEO, and facts you can stand behind.

Signals we work on

  • One canonical website that says who you are
  • Structured data with stable identifiers
  • Profiles that agree with each other
  • Clear person, company, and author relationships
  • Independent sources that corroborate the facts

Knowledge Panel readiness

We improve the evidence. Google makes the decision.

Google Knowledge Panels are generated automatically from information Google finds across the web. There is no legitimate form an SEO agency can submit to force Google to create one.

Our work focuses on the underlying evidence: the canonical site, the structured data, the profiles, the relationships, and the independent references that let Google recognize one entity and tell it apart from everyone else with a similar name.

If Google creates a Knowledge Panel, we can also help the verified entity review it and submit appropriate corrections.

Third-party corroboration

Your website cannot corroborate itself

Structured data can clarify what you say about yourself. Independent sources help corroborate it. When appropriate, an entity audit identifies gaps in credible third-party coverage, professional profiles, industry references, and other external evidence.

Lucky Domains does not manufacture fake coverage or represent paid placements as independent journalism. Press outreach is not part of this service unless it is separately scoped, and no number of articles guarantees Knowledge Graph inclusion.
