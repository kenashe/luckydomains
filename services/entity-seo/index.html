What entity SEO is

Your website can rank and still be misunderstood.

Traditional SEO helps pages rank. Entity SEO helps search engines understand the person or organization behind those pages: the canonical website, official profiles, founders, companies, authors, credentials, products, and credible references that all belong to the same real-world entity.

When those signals conflict or stay disconnected, systems struggle to tell one person or company from another. When they are consistent and machine-readable, they have a clearer picture to work with.

The evidence we work on

  • A clear canonical website
  • Accurate structured data
  • Consistent external profiles
  • Unambiguous person and company relationships
  • Credible third-party references
  • Consistent public facts

What we look at

Identity work compares what you say about yourself, in words and in data, everywhere you say it.

  • Organization and Person structured data

    Markup with stable identifiers that describes real, visible facts.

  • Identity consistency

    The same name, logo, and description everywhere.

  • Official profiles

    Links to official profiles only, and profiles that link back.

  • Founder relationships

    Founder, company, and author relationships stated in plain words and in data.

  • Your primary online identity (entity home)

    One page that says, authoritatively, who you are.

  • Telling you apart (entity disambiguation)

    What distinguishes your brand from people and companies with similar names.

Emerging research

For AI agents

Agents may need the same things people and companies do: a readable name, a way to be found, and a way to check who stands behind them. This is research, not a service.

Read the research article

How this differs from AI visibility. Entity SEO establishes who you are, stated the same way everywhere. AI visibility builds on that identity and improves the content and evidence systems use when they answer questions about it. Both depend on sound SEO. See AI Visibility & Brand Authority.

What the service includes

Two levels, built on facts you already have.

All structured data we write describes real facts already supported by you and your site.

Level 1

Entity foundation

The audit and fixes most people and companies need first.

  • Review of your primary online identity (entity home)
  • Person or Organization structured-data review
  • Stable entity @id architecture
  • sameAs profile mapping
  • Logo, image, name, and description consistency
  • Brand-search and entity-search baseline
Level 2

Entity authority

For larger or more complicated public footprints. Everything in the foundation, plus:

  • Cross-domain entity graph audit
  • Founder, company, author, and product relationship review
  • External profile and citation consistency audit
  • Third-party corroboration assessment
  • AI and LLM entity-discovery review
  • Knowledge Graph and brand-SERP monitoring recommendations

Named engagement. Either level can be scoped as an Entity and AI Visibility Audit: a baseline and plan for a person or company, agreed in writing before work starts.

Knowledge Panels. Google creates Knowledge Panels automatically. No one can submit a form to force one. This work improves the evidence Google uses to recognize an entity and tell it apart from everyone with a similar name.
