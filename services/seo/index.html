What SEO covers

The fundamentals still decide who gets found.

Technical health, pages matched to real search intent, useful content, and earned authority. AI-powered search draws on the same foundation, so this work now does two jobs.

Honest reporting. Reporting is plain English and tied to the work done: what changed, what moved, and what did not. There are no ranking guarantees, and no one who offers them should be trusted.

What we look at

An audit reads the site the way a search engine does, then the way a customer does.

  • Crawlability

    Whether search engines can reach every important page, with nothing important blocked.

  • Indexing

    Which pages are indexed, which are not, and why.

  • Internal linking

    Links that point to canonical URLs and show which pages matter.

  • Search intent

    Whether each page targets one clear thing a searcher is trying to do.

  • Technical errors

    Broken links, redirect chains, duplicate URLs, and slow templates.

  • Content structure

    Titles, headings, and copy that answer the question the page is for.

  • Local search signals

    Google Business Profile, consistent listings, and reviews, for businesses that serve a place.

  • Search Console data

    Queries, coverage, and changes over time, read before anything is changed.

Why SEO is expanding

AI search is built on search.

What the search engines themselves say.

Source: Google Search Central

Google says the same SEO fundamentals apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode: crawlable pages, useful first-party content, internal links, good page experience, and structured data that matches the visible text. It also says there is no special schema and no new AI text file required.

Read the source
Source: Bing Webmaster Tools

Bing Webmaster Tools now includes an AI Performance report that shows which pages are cited in AI-generated answers in Copilot and Bing, and the grounding queries behind those citations. AI visibility is becoming measurable, at least in part.

How we work

Five steps, repeated.

Audit

Crawl the site, read Search Console, and find what blocks indexing and ranking.

Fix the foundation

Speed, indexing, canonicals, structured data, and mobile health.

Target the pages

Match each page to a real query and the intent behind it.

Build content

Pages and articles that answer the questions buyers ask.

Monitor

Track what changed, report it honestly, and adjust.

First-party example

An internal system: the Autonomous SEO Agent

The Autonomous SEO Agent is an internal system the founder built. Each day it researches listing and profile sites, creates a profile where a listing is legitimate, free, and relevant, and confirms its own verification emails. Its public build record reports 63 sites researched and eight profiles confirmed live. Most of those profiles carry nofollow links, and no SEO impact has been established.
