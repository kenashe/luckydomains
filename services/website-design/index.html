The approach

Great design. Strong foundations.

A website should look great, reflect your brand, and make it easy for customers to understand what you offer. Lucky Domains combines thoughtful visual design, intuitive navigation, responsive development, and strong technical foundations so your website performs as well as it looks.

Design and structure are planned together. Typography, layout, and a consistent visual identity carry your brand. Clear page structure, fast templates, and accessible markup make the site easy to use and easy for search engines to read.

What a build includes

  • Modern visual design that reflects your brand
  • Typography, layout, and a consistent visual identity
  • Responsive pages, designed for phones first
  • Intuitive navigation and clear calls to action
  • Interactive elements and purposeful animation
  • Search and technical foundations built in
  • Accessibility and performance
  • Launched on your own domain, with a handoff you can edit

What we look at

The same things matter whether we review the site you have or design a new one.

  • Visual design

    Whether the site looks current and reflects your brand consistently.

  • Typography and layout

    Readable type, a clear hierarchy, and room to breathe on every screen.

  • User experience and navigation

    Whether visitors can find what they need, with important pages a click or two from the home page.

  • Calls to action and forms

    A clear next step on every page, and forms that are easy to complete.

  • Interaction and motion

    Interactive elements and animation that help explain, and that respect reduced-motion settings.

  • Responsive design

    Templates that work on every screen size, starting with phones.

  • Accessibility and performance

    Keyboard support, readable contrast, fast templates, and nothing loaded that the page does not need.

  • Search and technical foundations

    One address per page, internal links and the sitemap in agreement, and structured data that matches the visible text.

  • Maintainability

    A site you can edit yourself, documented in plain English.

How a project runs

Six steps, from discovery to improvement.

Discover

Goals, audience, the questions customers ask, and what the current site does and does not do.

Structure

A page plan built from the services you sell and what your customers search for.

Design

Visual design, typography, and layout that reflect your brand, make the business clear in seconds, and work on every screen size.

Build

Clean, maintainable implementation with performance, accessibility, and structured data built in.

Launch

On your domain, with redirects, canonicals, Search Console, and analytics set up correctly.

Improve

Measure what people and search engines do with the site, and adjust.

AI-assisted, human-reviewed. AI-assisted development and testing: research, page drafts, and repetitive markup move faster, and every change is checked by automated tests for links, metadata, accessibility, and performance before it ships. Ken reviews the result.

First-party example

How our own sites are built

LuckyDomains.io moved from Wix to plain static HTML on the company's own domain: one canonical URL per page, 93 internal links rewritten to match, and a test suite that runs on every change. The decisions are public. On KenAshe.ai, Ken's own site, 2,083 PNG images were migrated to WebP with responsive images.
