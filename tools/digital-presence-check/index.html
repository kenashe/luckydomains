Digital Presence Check

Ten questions about your domain, website, search visibility, identity, and AI visibility. It takes about two minutes and points to areas worth a closer look.

This is a preliminary self-check, not a technical audit. Nothing you select is stored or sent. The check runs entirely in your browser.

Domain

1We control the domain our business runs on, in an account the business owns.

You do not need an exact-match domain. You need a suitable one that you control.

2The domain is easy to say and spell, and our email uses it.

A name people can repeat, and the same name on your email, both build trust.

Website

3Within a few seconds, a new visitor can tell what we do, who we serve, and how to reach us.

Clarity comes before design.

4The site loads quickly and works well on a phone.

Many first visits happen on a phone.

Search

5We appear in search results when someone searches for our business name.

If your own name does not find you, something basic is in the way.

6We appear for the main services we offer, in the places we serve.

This is how people who do not know your name yet find you.

Identity

7Our name, description, and contact details match across our website and official profiles.

When sources disagree, search engines and AI systems hesitate.

8Our website includes Organization or Person structured data that matches what the page says.

If you are not sure, that is common. It is quick to check.

AI visibility

9We have checked how AI assistants describe our business.

A few minutes with the assistants your customers use is enough to start.

10When AI assistants describe us, the description is accurate.

Choose Not sure if you have not checked yet.
